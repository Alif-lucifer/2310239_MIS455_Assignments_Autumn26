Doraemon Movies

Doraemon has appeared in many animated movies. The main movie series has 45 theatrical films, beginning with Doraemon: Nobita's Dinosaur in 1980. Each movie takes Doraemon, Nobita and their friends on a different adventure.

Doraemon movies usually include adventure, friendship, teamwork and comedy. The characters travel to different places and sometimes different worlds, where they use their courage, teamwork and gadgets to overcome challenges.

You can explore more information about Doraemon movies on Wikipedia .
