About Doraemon

Doraemon is a robotic cat from the 22nd century. He travels back in time to help Nobita and improve his future. Doraemon uses different futuristic gadgets to help him.

The Doraemon series focuses on friendship, family, school life and adventure. The stories are entertaining and also show the importance of helping and caring for others.

Dorami is Doraemon's younger sister and is another important character in the series. She also has many useful gadgets.

Doraemon has become a well-known Japanese animated character and his stories are enjoyed by audiences in many countries.
