Doraemon's Amazing Gadgets

One of the most exciting parts of Doraemon is his collection of futuristic gadgets. Doraemon keeps many of these gadgets inside his special fourth-dimensional pocket.These gadgets can help Nobita travel to different places, communicate with others and solve different problems. They are an important part of Nobita & Doraemon's adventures.

Anywhere Door

The Anywhere Door is one of Doraemon's most famous gadgets. It allows people to travel to different places by simply opening the door.

Time Machine

The Time Machine allows Doraemon and his friends to travel through different periods of time and experience exciting adventures.

Bambo-copter

The Bambo-copter is a small flying gadget. It allows Doraemon and his friends to fly through the sky.
